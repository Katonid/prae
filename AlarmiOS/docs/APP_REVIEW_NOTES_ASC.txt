PURPOSE
Schulalarm is a free in-house alert app for school staff, offered to ANY school. Whoever installs it sets up their own school in a minute and invites colleagues with a six-character code; schools are separate, with nothing shared and nothing to buy. One teacher raises an alarm (intruder, fire, medical, or a drill); colleagues' iPads show what happened and where and offer two one-tap replies. It does NOT replace the emergency services and says so in the app.

NOTIFICATIONS ARE OPTIONAL (Guideline 4.5.4)
Your finding on build 42 was correct: it greyed out the "finish setup" button while the notification permission was missing, so a reviewer who declined the dialog could not leave that screen. Fixed, and further than the button:

- Nothing is gated on that permission any more. The blocking mechanism is gone from the source; the checklist is a report, not a gate, and "Einrichtung abschliessen" (finish setup) is always enabled.
- Consent is asked for inside the app, in a section "Mitteilungen - freiwillig" (notifications - optional): what they are for, that the app stays usable without them, and a screen listing what works without them. The dialog is only ever raised by an explicit tap, never at launch, and the section is in Settings too, so somebody who declined can change their mind.

Without notifications everything still works - raising an alarm, seeing a running one, acknowledging, the chat, the all clear, administration, join codes, diagnostics. The app polls every five seconds while an alarm runs, whatever the permission, so an open app shows an alarm within seconds. Lost is only sound while the app is in the background, the reminders and the sound test.

The camera follows the same rule: the scanner asks at the point of use; if denied it says so and points at the way round, since the code can be typed in.

HOW TO TEST - no test account or join code needed
1. Sign in to iCloud; the app stores everything in CloudKit.
2. Choose "Schule einrichten" (set up a school), NOT "Beitreten" (join). Any school name and any handle, e.g. TEST, then "Schule einrichten und Admin werden". This matters: whoever sets up a school is its administrator, and only an administrator may raise a drill.
3. Either answer on notifications continues the review. To hear an alarm, tap "Mitteilungen erlauben" and allow; iOS then asks again for critical alerts, optional too, which only decides audibility on a silenced device.
4. No checklist item blocks anything. "Zustellung geprueft" (delivery verified) stays red on a single device: CloudKit does not push to the device that wrote the record, so one device cannot prove delivery to itself. Tap "Einrichtung abschliessen".
5. Tap "Alarm ausloesen", choose "PROBEALARM" (drill), pick a location, let the five-second countdown run. Acknowledge with "Gesehen - Klasse gesichert", then end it with "Entwarnung geben". Please use the drill type.
6. Optional and local: Einstellungen -> "Tontest" plays the alarm as a notification about 8 seconds later. Lock the device after tapping - that is the point.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
During an alarm, colleagues send short messages visible only inside their own school's group. Every message has a "..." menu with "Diese Nachricht melden" (report), opening a mail to schulalarm@apps.dblern.de with sender, timestamp and wording filled in; that address is also in Einstellungen -> Hilfe and on the support page. An administrator removes a member under Verwaltung -> Mitglieder and revokes the join code. Records go after 90 days.

ACCOUNTS
No account of our own: the app uses the device's iCloud account and asks for no e-mail, password or personal data. It creates a membership (a handle in one school's list); Einstellungen -> "Verbindung zur Schule loesen" deletes it and this device's subscriptions.

The instruction texts shown during an alarm say on purpose that they are not final: what a school tells its staff must be agreed locally. Critical alerts: granted 08.09.2026.
